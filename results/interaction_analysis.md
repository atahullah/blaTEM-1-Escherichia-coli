# Interaction Analysis

## Overview

The structural effects of the I84V and V184A mutations were evaluated using DynaMut2. The interactions observed in the mutant proteins were compared with those of the wild-type TEM-1 β-lactamase structure (PDB ID: 1BTL).

## Interaction Types Evaluated

- Hydrogen bonds
- Hydrophobic interactions
- Van der Waals interactions
- Polar interactions
- Aromatic interactions
- Carbonyl interactions
- Ionic interactions
- Steric clashes

## I84V Mutation

The I84V mutation altered the local interaction network surrounding residue 84. Differences were observed between the wild-type and mutant structures in hydrophobic, hydrogen-bond, and van der Waals interactions.

## V184A Mutation

The V184A mutation also modified the interaction network around residue 184. Changes in hydrogen bonding, hydrophobic contacts, van der Waals interactions, and polar interactions were observed relative to the wild-type structure.

## Interpretation

The interaction changes suggest that both mutations influence the local structural environment of TEM-1 β-lactamase. These structural differences may contribute to altered protein stability and function.
