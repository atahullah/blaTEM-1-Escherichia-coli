# Protein Stability Analysis

## Software

DynaMut2

## Protein Structure

- PDB ID: 1BTL
- Organism: *Escherichia coli*
- Protein: TEM-1 β-lactamase

## Mutations Analyzed

- I84V
- V184A

## Method

Protein stability analysis was performed using the DynaMut2 web server. The wild-type protein structure (PDB ID: 1BTL) was used as the reference. Each mutation was analyzed independently to evaluate its effect on protein stability and molecular interactions.

## Parameters Evaluated

- Protein stability (ΔΔG)
- Hydrogen bonds
- Hydrophobic interactions
- Van der Waals interactions
- Polar interactions
- Steric clashes
- Other residue interactions

## Results

The stability and interaction changes observed for the I84V and V184A mutations were compared with the wild-type protein. The detailed interaction figures are available in the `dynamut2` folder.

## Conclusion

The computational analysis provided insight into the structural effects of both mutations and their potential influence on the stability and function of TEM-1 β-lactamase.
